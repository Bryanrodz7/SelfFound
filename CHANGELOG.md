# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First public release.

- Self-found tracking per character, started automatically on fresh level 1 characters.
- Detects trades, taking items or gold from mail, and auction house purchases.
- Tamper checks: signed save data and /played comparison on every login.
- Peer verification over guild, party/raid, and an optional server-wide channel.
- Badge, player list panel, tooltips, warnings and alerts.
- Settings for badge and window size, alerts, sounds and network mode.
- Slash commands: `/sf`, `/sf settings`, `/sf reset`, `/sf list`.
