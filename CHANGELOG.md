# Changelog

## v0.1.1-beta

- First release built automatically from GitHub.
- Players are matched by character ID, so first and last names are handled correctly.
- Added support for WoW Forever.

## v0.1.0

First public release.

- Self-found tracking per character, started automatically on fresh level 1 characters.
- Detects trades, taking items or gold mailed by other players, and auction house purchases. Selling on the auction house and NPC mail are allowed.
- Tamper checks: signed save data and /played comparison on every login.
- Peer verification over guild, party/raid, and an optional server-wide channel, or solo mode with sharing off.
- Badge, player list panel, tooltips, warnings and alerts.
- Settings for badge and window size, alerts, sounds and network mode.
- Slash commands: `/sf`, `/sf settings`, `/sf reset`, `/sf list`.
